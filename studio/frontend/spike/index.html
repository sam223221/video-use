Can this phone edit video by itself?

Four short tests, all on this phone — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Do them top to bottom; each one saves its result as it goes, so nothing is lost even if the page crashes. At the end, copy the results and send them to Sam.

You'll need: one big video (the bigger the better — around 1 GB is perfect) that is BOTH in your Photos and saved to the Files app. To save it to Files: open it in Photos → Share → Save to Files.

Heads up: this browser is blocking saved results (private browsing?). The tests still work, but results will NOT survive closing the page — copy them before you leave.
Please keep the screen ON and stay on this page while the test runs — this phone won't keep it awake automatically.

1Pick the same clip two ways

Why: when a web page asks for a video, iPhone may quietly hand over a smaller, converted copy instead of the real file. This test shows whether that still happens, by comparing the two ways of picking.

  1. Tap Pick from Photo Library. In the menu that appears choose Photo Library, then select your big clip and wait — if the phone shows “Preparing…”, that's it converting.
  2. Tap Pick from Files. In the menu choose Choose File, then find the SAME clip where you saved it in the Files app.
  3. Look at the comparison that appears — differences are highlighted.

2Copy it in, save it back out

Why: editing on the phone means the browser must hold its own working copy of the video, and afterwards save the result back into Photos. This test does a full round trip with your big clip.

  1. Tap Start the copy and wait — for a 1 GB clip this can take a while. Keep the screen on.
  2. When it finishes, tap Save to Photos. In the share sheet that opens, scroll down and tap Save Video.
  3. Open the Photos app, find the saved video, and check it plays. Then come back and tick the box.

First pick a clip in test 1 (the Files pick is the right one — it's the original).

3Cut out a 30-second piece

Why: this is the heart of the future editor — cutting a piece out of a big video WITHOUT re-encoding it (no quality loss, and it should be fast). The cut is taken from around the middle of the clip and saved as a new video.

  1. Tap Cut a 30-second piece and wait. The page then CHECKS the result file itself and tells you whether it came out healthy.
  2. Tap Save the cut to Photos, then Save Video in the share sheet.
  3. Open Photos, play the cut — check picture AND sound — then tick the box.

Heads up: the sound may stop a second or two before the picture does at the very end of the cut — that is expected (the picture has to end on a clean frame boundary, so it runs slightly past where the sound stops), not a bug.

First pick a clip in test 1, or run test 2 (its copy can be used).
